# Translation quality and review checklist

## Priority languages
1. Hindi
2. Urdu
3. Malayalam

## Automated/content consistency checks completed for this draft
- The Hindi and Urdu step instructions and dua meanings are present.
- Malayalam step instructions, dua text, transliteration and meanings are present in the main guide.
- Arabic source duas are retained rather than translated over or altered.
- The language selector exposes only the priority Indian languages plus English and Arabic.
- Urdu is displayed right-to-left.
- English remains available as a reference language.

## Still required before claiming full verification
- Independent native-speaker proofreading for Hindi, Urdu and Malayalam.
- Review of Islamic terminology and ritual descriptions by a qualified, trusted scholar.
- Check Arabic spelling/diacritics and each hadith reference against authoritative editions.
- Test the published website on Android and iPhone, including print-to-PDF.

Do not label the translations “scholar approved” or “fully verified” until those human reviews have actually taken place.

7 Important Duas

Selected from the Qur’an and authentic Sunnah, with Arabic, pronunciation, meaning and source references.

Important note: These are seven selected supplications and remembrances—not a compulsory checklist. You do not have to recite all seven, and no special dua is prescribed for each Tawaf circuit. Make personal dua in any language. Transliteration is only a pronunciation aid; learn the Arabic from a qualified teacher.

1. Talbiyah — after entering Ihram

لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ

Pronunciation: Labbayka Allahumma labbayk. Labbayka la sharika laka labbayk. Innal-hamda wan-ni‘mata laka wal-mulk, la sharika lak.

Meaning: Here I am, O Allah, here I am. You have no partner; here I am. Indeed, all praise, blessings and dominion belong to You. You have no partner.

Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 1549; also Sahih Muslim 1184. Read hadith.

2. Entering the mosque — including Masjid al-Haram

اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ

Pronunciation: Allahummaftah li abwaba rahmatik.

Meaning: O Allah, open for me the gates of Your mercy.

Source: Sahih Muslim 713a. Read hadith.

3. Asking for good in both worlds

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Pronunciation: Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina ‘adhaban-nar.

Meaning: Our Lord, grant us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.

Source: Qur’an 2:201. Anas reported that this was among the Prophet’s most frequent supplications: Sahih al-Bukhari 6389. Read Qur’an 2:201 · Read hadith.

4. Dua of Musa — asking Allah for good

رَبِّ إِنِّي لِمَا أَنْزَلْتَ إِلَيَّ مِنْ خَيْرٍ فَقِيرٌ

Pronunciation: Rabbi inni lima anzalta ilayya min khayrin faqir.

Meaning: My Lord, I am truly in need of whatever good You send down to me.

Source: Qur’an 28:24 (the supplication of Prophet Musa). Read Qur’an 28:24.

5. Dua of Yunus — in distress

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ

Pronunciation: La ilaha illa anta, subhanaka, inni kuntu minaz-zalimin.

Meaning: There is no deity except You; glory be to You. I have indeed been among the wrongdoers.

Source: Qur’an 21:87–88. Read Qur’an 21:87–88.

6. Asking for guidance, piety and sufficiency

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْهُدَى وَالتُّقَى وَالْعَفَافَ وَالْغِنَى

Pronunciation: Allahumma inni as’alukal-huda wat-tuqa wal-‘afafa wal-ghina.

Meaning: O Allah, I ask You for guidance, piety, chastity and self-sufficiency.

Source: Sahih Muslim 2721. Read hadith.

7. Seeking protection from worry, grief and debt

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْبُخْلِ وَالْجُبْنِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ

Pronunciation: Allahumma inni a‘udhu bika minal-hammi wal-hazan, wal-‘ajzi wal-kasal, wal-bukhli wal-jubn, wa dala‘id-dayni wa ghalabatir-rijal.

Meaning: O Allah, I seek refuge in You from anxiety and sorrow, incapacity and laziness, miserliness and cowardice, the burden of debt and being overpowered by people.

Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 6369. Read hadith.
